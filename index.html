Our kashrut

Uncompromising, from the shechita to your table.

Kashrut is not a label we add at the end — it is how every animal is chosen, every cut is prepared, and every order is packed.

I

Glatt Beit Yosef

Only animals whose lungs are completely free of adhesions (חלק), according to the ruling of the Beit Yosef — the strictest Sephardic standard.

II

Sephardic Nikkur Achoraim

The hindquarter is porged by expert menakrim — removing the chelev and gid hanasheh — according to Sephardic custom, so we can offer filet, striploin, picanha and leg of lamb.

III

Badatz Mekor Chaim

Every stage — shechita, bedika, nikkur, soaking and salting — under the supervision of Badatz Mekor Chaim.
